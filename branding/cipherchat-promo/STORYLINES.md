# CipherChat promo — three storylines (20 s, 9:16, 1080×1920, 30 fps)

Facts the film may use (from the app's code and docs, nothing else):
- Chat with contacts and groups, delivered in real time ("Delivered" only after the other phone confirms).
- Tap the cap icon in the composer to send a message as a cipher: Caesar, Vigenère, ROT13, Substitution, Custom alphabet, Emoji code. The key is never sent; the friend long-presses → "Decode…" and types it.
- Disappearing messages: 10 s, 1 min, 1 h, 24 h.
- Honest: ciphers are for fun, not security. The film never says secure, private, protected or encrypted.

Look for all three: the app's own dark theme. Charcoal-green ground #111513 with its slow emerald colour fields, emerald #2ECC8F as the one accent, mint text #E4EAE6, frosted glass on floating bars (the brand's own material), serif for message text, sans for UI. UI pieces rebuilt from the app's code at 1.6–2× size. Logo from `branding/logo/masters`.

## A — "Meet at 7" (recommended)

**Device:** a moment in someone's day. **Form:** object-to-object chain, where each shot ends on the thing that opens the next.

A friend group is planning tonight. Maya taps the cap icon, picks Caesar, and "Meet at 7" scrambles into "Phhw dw 7" as it flies into a bubble. Sam long-presses it, types the key 3, and the letters shuffle back into the plan. Same message, one secret.

- **First 2 s:** the chat already open and filling the frame, Maya's thumb on the cap icon, and the letters of "Meet at 7" starting to shift one by one.
- **Beats:** hook (letters shift, 0–2 s) → send (the bubble flies up, "Delivered", 2–5 s) → Sam's phone (the coded bubble arrives, 5–8 s) → decode (long-press, key 3, letters flip back, 8–13 s) → the other ciphers (the same message cycles through Vigenère, ROT13 and Emoji code inside the one bubble, 13–16 s) → end card (the bubble splits into the Twin Bubbles mark, which swings 180° into place, then "Chat in code. Just for fun." 16–20 s).
- **Sound:** a plucked synth motif in a minor key, ticks on each letter flip, a soft whoosh on send, a chord on the decode.

## B — "A → D"

**Device:** type carries the argument. **Form:** cut on the beat.

Giant letters fill the screen and step through the alphabet on every beat: A becomes D, HELLO becomes KHOOR, cipher names slam in one per bar, and the emoji code turns words into animals. It ends with the circle of a cipher wheel becoming the logo.

- **First 2 s:** a single huge "A" sliding three places to "D", then "HELLO" rippling into "KHOOR".
- **Beats:** shift (0–4 s) → six ciphers, one per beat, each transforming the same word (4–12 s) → into the app (the word lands inside a chat bubble, 12–15 s) → wheel → logo (15–20 s).
- **Sound:** a 120 BPM beat, a snare on every shift, a riser into the logo.

## C — "The bubble"

**Device:** the object is the explanation. **Form:** an endless zoom.

One chat bubble is the whole film. We dive into its text, which turns into a cipher wheel. Inside the wheel's centre are the emoji codes, and inside one emoji is a disappearing message ticking away. Pulling all the way out, the bubble and its twin form the logo.

- **First 2 s:** a bubble fills the frame and the camera is already falling into the gap of its letters.
- **Beats:** bubble → wheel (0–6 s) → emoji grid (6–11 s) → disappearing timer (11–15 s) → pull-out reveal of the mark (15–20 s).
- **Sound:** a continuous rising pad with a tick at every zoom threshold.
